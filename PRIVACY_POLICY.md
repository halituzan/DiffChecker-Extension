# Privacy Policy for Diff Checker

**Last updated:** June 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Diff Checker** ("the Extension," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you install and use our browser extension. Diff Checker is a line-by-line text comparison tool for Chromium-based browsers (including Chrome, Opera, Edge, and Brave).

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at [halituzan.com](https://halituzan.com) or via the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/halituzan/DiffChecker-Extension).

---

## Summary

Diff Checker is designed to work **entirely on your device**. We do **not** collect, sell, rent, or share your personal data. The text you compare never leaves your browser unless you choose to copy or export it yourself.

---

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do **not** collect, transmit, or store on external servers:

- The text you enter or paste for comparison
- Diff results or comparison history
- Browsing history or website content
- Account credentials or payment information
- Analytics, usage statistics, or telemetry
- Device identifiers for tracking or advertising

---

## Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Extension may save a small number of **preferences** on your device so your settings persist between sessions. This data stays in your browser and is not sent to us or any third party.

| Data | Where it is stored | Purpose |
|------|-------------------|---------|
| Open mode (new tab or popup) | Browser extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) | Remembers how you prefer the Extension to open |
| Theme (light/dark) | Browser local storage | Remembers your visual theme choice |
| Interface language | Browser local storage | Remembers your selected UI language |

You can clear this data at any time by removing the Extension or clearing extension/site data in your browser settings.

---

## How Text Comparison Works

When you compare two texts:

1. You enter or paste content into the Extension interface.
2. The comparison runs **locally in your browser** using built-in JavaScript.
3. Results are displayed on your screen.

No comparison data is uploaded to our servers or any third-party service because we do not operate such services for this Extension.

---

## Permissions

The Extension requests only the permissions it needs to function:

- **`storage`** — Saves your open-mode preference (new tab vs. popup) locally in browser extension storage.

The Extension does **not** request broad permissions such as access to all websites, tabs, browsing history, clipboard (beyond normal user-initiated copy actions), or network-wide data collection.

---

## Third-Party Services

Diff Checker does **not** integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

The Extension interface may include optional links to external websites (for example, project source code or developer profiles). Those links open only when **you** click them. We do not receive data from those sites, and their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

---

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do **not** sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties.

We would only disclose information if required to do so by law or in the good-faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with legal obligations, protect rights, or ensure safety—though, as noted above, we do not collect user content or personal data through the Extension.

---

## Data Retention

- **Text you compare:** Not retained by us. It exists only in your browser session while you use the Extension, unless you save it elsewhere yourself.
- **Local preferences:** Retained until you change them, clear browser/extension data, or uninstall the Extension.

---

## Children's Privacy

Diff Checker is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not collect personal data through the Extension, no special child-data processing occurs.

---

## Security

All processing happens locally in your browser. While no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, limiting data to your device and avoiding external transmission reduces exposure compared with cloud-based tools.

---

## International Users

Because data is processed locally on your device and not transmitted to our servers, the Extension generally does not involve cross-border data transfers by us. Your browser and operating system may still process data according to their own settings and jurisdiction.

---

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the Extension after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

For significant changes, we may also update the policy in the Extension's store listing or repository.

---

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding personal data (such as access, correction, or deletion). Because Diff Checker does not collect or store your personal data on our systems, most such requests will not apply to data held by us. You can always:

- Clear local Extension preferences via your browser settings
- Uninstall the Extension to remove locally stored Extension data
